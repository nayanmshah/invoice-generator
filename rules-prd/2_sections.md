# PRD 15 Mandatory Sections (Template v2)

All 15 sections must be present in this exact order. See `.nayan/guidance/prd-template-v2.md` for full structure and content requirements.

1. Basic Information (metadata, Sign-Off table)
2. Problem Statement
3. KPIs & Business Justification
4. Success Criteria
5. Users & Personas
6. Scope
7. Use Cases (each with Steps, Acceptance Criteria; include Business Rules, Validation Rules, Calculation Logic, State Transitions where applicable)
8. Data, Entities & Information
9. Integrations
10. Error Handling & Edge Cases
11. Non-Functional Expectations
12. UI/UX Requirements
13. Constraints & Dependencies
14. Open Questions
15. Appendix (Glossary/Domain Glossary, References)

**Concluding phrase:** "PRD Complete - Ready for architect and UI/UX (prototype)."
